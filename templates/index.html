Understand the change. Explore the possibilities. Act earlier.

📈

Problem Statement

Healthcare decisions often rely on single-point measurements or population averages that may not reflect an individual's unique health trajectory. Patients and clinicians lack tools to understand how personal baselines evolve over time and how hypothetical interventions might affect health outcomes.

Swasthya Twin addresses this by creating a patient-specific virtual health model that maintains longitudinal observations, computes personalized risk assessments, and enables evidence-based scenario exploration—all with transparent explanations and clear uncertainty communication.

System Architecture

A modular, evidence-oriented architecture for reliable Digital Twin computation.

1
Data Ingestion: Manual entry, CSV import, or synthetic data generation with validation and provenance tracking
2
Persistence Layer: SQLite database with patient profiles, observations, scenarios, and provenance ledger
3
Digital Twin Engine: Computes baseline values, trends, rolling averages, and data quality metrics
4
Prediction Engine: Rules-based risk assessment with explainable feature contributions
5
Scenario Engine: Hypothetical simulation with modified features and outcome comparison
6
Evidence Export: FHIR-compatible mappings, summary reports, and downloadable evidence bundles

⚠️ Research & Safety Disclaimer

Swasthya Twin is a research prototype and demonstration software. It is NOT a clinical diagnostic tool, does NOT provide medical advice, and has NOT been validated for clinical use. Risk assessments are based on rules-based screening of available observations and should not be used for medical decision-making. Always consult qualified healthcare professionals for health-related decisions. This system demonstrates Digital Twin concepts for educational and research purposes only.
